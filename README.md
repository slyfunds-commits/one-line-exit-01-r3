Round 3 (final) entry for [The One-Line Exit](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kreutzx-the-one-line-exit-1dc652d1) — a one-line resignation letter.

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no third-party packages) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I resign: HR approved it in 4 seconds, 4 seconds longer than my interview lasted.

HR approved the resignation in four seconds — four seconds longer than the interview lasted. The approval's speed is the insult: the same mechanism that approves in four seconds is the one that never really interviewed me. The line's second beat is the interview itself, which lasted about nothing — the company never met me at all, and the reader completes that step. Round 1 scored 61.23% with a family-fabrication line and Round 2 swapped down to 52.25% on a logic turn; this round turns on a fresh device instead: the instant acceptance, measured in specific non-round seconds, with the repeated "4 seconds" echoing from approval-time to interview-time.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The entry is exactly one line, 81 characters, pure ASCII, with no trailing newline.
